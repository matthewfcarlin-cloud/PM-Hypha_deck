Competitive analysis · SWOT

Where we’re strong, and where we’re exposed.

StrengthsInternal · helpful
  • The only option we found that is both made on demand and home compostable [16]–[22]
  • Custom fit for every box with no molds; molded mycelium needs $2K–$9K tooling [20]
  • Plastic-free material: mushroom roots plus farm waste
WeaknessesInternal · harmful
  • No prototype yet: expansion, set time and strength are untested
  • Cost per order unknown; Holiday pays ~$1 an order and caps a switch at 2× [15]
  • Mycelium grows slowly (7 days for molded parts), so the paste must be pre-grown [20]
OpportunitiesExternal · helpful
  • US & Canada void fill: $1.9B in 2025, growing to $3.2B by 2035 [1]
  • E-commerce is the largest use, at 42.1% of all void fill [2]
  • EPR laws (CA, OR, WA) and California’s 25% single-use plastic cut by 2032 push brands off plastic [1] [23]
ThreatsExternal · harmful
  • Incumbents already run fill machines at the pack line; the top 3 hold 18% of the market [3] [16] [17]
  • Ranpak sizes paper fill with AI vision; Packsize’s right-sized boxes cut the need for fill [18] [22]
  • Paper fill is cheap and curbside recyclable, “green enough” for many buyers [17] [18]
Sources: [1] Future Market Insights, US & Canada Protective Packaging · [2] Market Intelo, Void Fill Packaging · [3] FMI, Void Fill Systems Market Share · [15] Holiday interview (Group 11) · [16] Sealed Air · [17] Pregis · [18] Ranpak · [20] Mushroom Packaging · [22] Packsize · [23] Grand View Research

Our value proposition

Packaging shouldn’t outlive the order.

Every Hypha fill is built on one belief: *what protects a product for a few days shouldn’t sit in a landfill for years.* So we print each fill from mushroom roots and farm waste, *shaped to the product and made to return to the soil.*
